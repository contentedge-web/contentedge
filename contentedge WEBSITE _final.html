Pro tip: Before/after is the most powerful story arc on LinkedIn. Specific numbers always outperform vague claims.
Tips
5 things every LinkedIn profile needs (most people miss #3):

1. A headline that speaks to your buyer, not your job title
2. A banner that shows your niche in 3 seconds
3. A first-person About section that tells a story
4. A Featured section pinned with your best content
5. At least 3 recent posts so visitors know you are active
Pro tip: End with a question. Questions boost comments by 60–80% and signal to LinkedIn that people should engage.
Hot Take
Unpopular opinion: Posting every day on LinkedIn is NOT the key to growth.

Everyone says consistency is king. Post daily. Build the habit.

Here is what I actually think: quality compounds. Noise gets ignored.

One deeply valuable post per week beats seven forgettable ones.
Pro tip: Do not soften your take. Hedging kills engagement. People who agree AND disagree both boost your reach.
CTA

→ [Benefit 2]
→ [Benefit 3]

[PRICE] · Instant download. Link in comments 👇
Pro tip: Put the link in COMMENTS — not the post body. LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links.
Lead Gen

I am putting together a free [GUIDE/CHECKLIST] on [TOPIC].

Pro tip: Comment CTAs get 3–5x more responses than link CTAs — they keep people on-platform and LinkedIn rewards that with wider reach.
